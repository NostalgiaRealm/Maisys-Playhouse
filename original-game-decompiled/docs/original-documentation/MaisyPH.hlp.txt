===== TOPIC 1: Activities =====


Activities



Maisy’s House

To play in Maisy’s house, you need to pick a room. Just click on the door of the kitchen, bathroom, or bedroom to go inside. Once your inside the rooms, take a look around at all of Maisy’s fun and interesting things. When you find something that highlights, click on it for a surprise.


Messy Room

Oh dear! What is all this mess in Maisy’s room? Help Maisy clean up her room by clicking on the things that need to be put away.



Level One and Two:
 Just click on the items to clean up Maisy’s house.

Level Three:
 Help Maisy clean up by putting the mess away properly.


Painting Pictures

What’s Maisy doing now? She’s painting pictures!

Level One: 
To help Maisy, you need to pick the picture that’s different from the others.

Level Two and Three: 
To help Maisy, you need to pick the picture that doesn’t belong with the others.


Dressing Up

What’s are you going to do today, Maisy? To help Maisy pick out the right outfit for the day, just click on the clothing in the wardrobe. When you pick the right thing, it will show up on Maisy. Keep going until Maisy is dressed.





Coloring

Follow the directions as you color in the pictures. Use the print button to print out your masterpiece.

Level One:
 Just listen to the directions and click on the thing you need to color in.

Level Two and Three:
 Listen to the directions, then dip the paintbrush into the color that you need. Once your paintbrush is the right color, go ahead and color!


Gingerbread

Maisy is making gingerbread cookies, but some of the cookies are broken! Put the cookies together like a puzzle. Then use the candies and frosting pieces to decorate the cookies. To print out your masterpiece, use the print button.





Train Station

Help Maisy board the animals on to the train.

Level One:
 Match the animals to the animals on the train car flags.

Level Two:
 Match the animal to the animal sound the train car makes.

Level Three:
 Groups of animals are waiting to get on the train. Match the number in the group, to the number on the train car.


Hide and Seek

What’s Maisy doing now? She’s playing hide and seek with everyone! Take a good look around and when you find someone, click on  them.

===== TOPIC 3: General Trouble Shooting =====


General Troubleshooting



If 
Maisy's Playhouse
 crashes, freezes, or gives an error message, reboot the computer after the problem occurs.



Many problems can be corrected checking the following:



System Requirements

Ensure sure that the computer meets or exceeds the 
system requirements
.The game may not run properly if the system is below the requirements.


CD-ROM Problems

If the 
Maisy's Playhouse
 is crashing, freezing, or reporting error messages, remove the CD from the drive and examine the shiny side. A scratched or dirty CD may cause problems. Clean the CD if needed.





Third Party Programs

Third party programs can potentially interfere with the operation 
Maisy's Playhouse
. If problems are occurring, make sure that no other programs are running at the same time. Close down any unnecessary programs and try again. If this does not work, hold down the 
Ctrl-Alt-Delete
 keys simultaneously to check what is running. End task for all programs except systray and explorer.




Drivers

Many video and sound problems are caused by driver problems. Contact the computer manufacturer and ensure that the sound and video drivers for the system are updated to the most recent versions. Updated drivers are generally available for free over the Internet, and can fix some bugs.





Video Mode

Although it will run in higher video modes, 
Maisy's Playhouse
 
runs best in 640x480, 256-color video mode. Click on the 
START
 button, then choose 
SETTINGS
, then choose 
CONTROL PANEL
. Double-click the icon for
 DISPLAY
, then click on the tab labeled 
SETTINGS
. Change the color palette to 256 colors and the desktop area to 640x480. Click the OK button. Follow the on-screen instructions to continue.






No Sound

Maisy's Playhouse
 operates using the standard Windows sound setup. Most sound problems can be traced to a problem with the Windows sound setup. First check that the Windows volume and mixer settings are set to proper levels. Second, make sure that the speakers are on, properly connected to the computer, and their volume is set properly. If this does not fix the problem, open a .wav sound file with the Windows Media Player. If an error is being encountered in playing back the sound, contact the computer manufacturer for support with the problem.




If the above steps do not fix the problem, please contact 
Product Support
 for further help.

Interface Buttons:




The Easel Button



Click on the Easel Button to choose an activity. Just roll the mouse over the buttons to hear the activities name. Click on any activity to play it.



The House Button



To quit an activity and return to Maisy’s House, choose the house button.



The Panda Button

Maisy’s Playhouse
.
 




===== TOPIC 5: Introduction =====


Introduction





Babe and his friends are gathering together to help support charitable organizations in Metropolis by staging a benefit circus performance. Join in the fun as Babe leads the effort to sell tickets to the show by helping out fellow Metropolis residents. When all of the tickets are sold, you can direct your own three-ring circus starring Babe and all of his friends from "Babe Pig In the City."

Throughout the activities you play in Metropolis, you will use the "click and stick" feature. This means you only need to click once on an item to pick it up, then click a second time to drop it. Listen carefully to your host, the Circus Ringmaster, for specific instructions for each activity. 



Let’s get on with the show!
